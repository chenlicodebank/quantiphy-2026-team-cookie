# q534 – width of the paper next to the magnifying glass

Scale (prior: glass falls at 9.8 m/s^2, frames 0-10, 24 fps = question_fps): own difference-mask track of the ring part
(measure.py). Quadratic fits of y(t): mask centroid 7393 px/s^2, bbox-mid 7389 / 7367 (frames 0-10 / 2-10); top and
bottom edges 7050-7690 (tilt changes the edge-on height). Record's bbox-centre second differences ~13 px/frame^2 = 7490.
Adopted 7390 px/s^2 -> 9.8/7390 = 1.326e-3 m/px. Sanity: glass length 162 px -> 0.215 m (plausible magnifier).

Paper: the white card lying on the desk immediately left of where the glass lands (front card, x 732-885, y ~997-1045,
same front-row depth as the landed glass). Its edges are axis-aligned (right edge x=884 at all rows); horizontal extent
152 px (bottom edge 732-884). Front-to-back extent is foreshortened (48 px), the real depth is larger, so the
horizontal 152 px is the shorter side = width: 152 x 1.326e-3 = 0.20 m.

Second route / alternative reading: the card behind the glass under the eyeglasses (x ~976-1108 visible, left end hidden
under the glasses, slightly farther back) gives >=132 px, i.e. >=0.175 m, consistent with ~0.2 m for the same card type.
Scale route check with record's accel (7490) gives 0.199 m.

Answer 0.20 m. Confidence moderate (which paper / which side is "width" is the main uncertainty).
